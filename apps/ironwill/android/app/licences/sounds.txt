Bundled sounds in this directory are derived from the freedesktop and KDE Oxygen
sound themes:

* soft_chime.ogg     <- freedesktop stereo/bell.oga, CC-BY 3.0,
                       Dr. Richard Boulanger et al.
* gong.ogg           <- freedesktop stereo/alarm-clock-elapsed.oga,
                       CC-BY-SA 3.0, Tim/corsica_s.
* deep_bell.ogg      <- KDE Oxygen stereo/bell-window-system.ogg,
                       CC-BY-SA 3.0.
* singing_bowl.ogg   <- freedesktop stereo/complete.oga, CC-BY 3.0,
                       Dr. Richard Boulanger et al.
